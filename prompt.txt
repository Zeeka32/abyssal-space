Build Astral Space: a real-time, fully procedural space exploration scene in the browser.

PRIORITIES
1. Visual quality and a coherent sense of scale.
2. Smooth free flight and stable frame pacing.
3. A small, offline, self-contained deliverable.

CONSTRAINTS
- Direct WebGPU. No Three.js, Babylon.js or other game engines.
- Every visual and sound is generated from code: no imported textures, models, audio or fonts.
- Deliver one self-contained index.html, generated from readable source.
- Seeded content generation, preallocated camera/instance state and reused frame resources.

SCENE
- A banded gas giant with tilted rings, gaps, debris and analytic ring shadows.
- A terrestrial planet with continents, oceans, polar ice, clouds and night lights.
- Cratered moons and rocky worlds, a volcanic planet and a frozen planet.
- A luminous star, a deterministic starfield and a procedural nebula background.
- Atmospheric rims, day/night lighting, planetary rotation and an orbiting asteroid belt.
- Seven destinations, including a procedural black hole with an accretion disk and stylized lensing.
- Planets start distributed around a central sun on a common ecliptic, with visible orbit guides; Selene orbits Aurelia. Rings and atmospheres follow moving centers.
- Destinations track their planet until manual flight starts; collision bounds follow orbital motion.
- Tonal ion-drive cruise and warp-charge boost audio, with no wind or noise buffers.
- A quiet observatory menu named Astral Space, with a footer credit linking to https://github.com/Noniv/abyssal-garden.
- Compressed artistic distances and orbital periods; no N-body gravity simulation.

CONTROLS
WASD fly, mouse look with pointer lock, Q/E down/up, Shift boost.
1-7 destinations, Esc menu, M mute, H statistics. Touch flight support.

VERIFICATION
- Compile and run all WGSL in actual Chrome WebGPU.
- Inspect seven destination screenshots and the menu.
- Check controls, synthesized audio startup, collisions and the camera route.
- Verify High, Medium and Low, plus the minified production file from disk.
- Record average, p99 and maximum frame/GPU times for the 60-second flight.
- Report measured hardware, resolution, bundle size and any verification limits honestly.

- Generation, GPU setup and simulation are gated by the first Start click.
- Fixed distant giant worlds and procedural spiral galaxies provide a vast background, with no destination buttons or solar orbits.
